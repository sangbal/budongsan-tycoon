Frequently Asked Questions

Gameplay

What is Seoul Survival?

Seoul Survival is a prestige-based incremental idle game set in modern Seoul. Start as a part-time worker earning ₩10,000 per click, get promoted through 10 career ranks (Part-timer → CEO), invest in financial products and real estate, and prestige by purchasing Seoul Tower for ₩1 trillion to earn permanent Career Points (CP).

How does the prestige system work?

When you accumulate enough wealth to buy Seoul Tower (₩1 trillion), you can prestige. This resets your money, investments, and career rank, but you earn Career Points (CP) that permanently multiply your income in the next run. Each prestige cycle makes you stronger.

What are the investment types?

There are two categories: Financial investments (Savings Account ₩50K, Fixed Deposit ₩500K, Korean Stocks ₩5M, US Stocks ₩25M, Cryptocurrency ₩100M) and Real Estate (Villa ₩250M, Officetel ₩350M, Apartment ₩800M, Commercial Shop ₩1.2B, Building ₩3B). Each generates passive income per second.

What is the career/rank system?

You start as a Part-timer (1x income multiplier) and can be promoted through 10 ranks by accumulating clicks: Part-timer(0) → Contract(100) → Employee(300) → Assistant Manager(700) → Manager(1,200) → Deputy Manager(2,000) → Director(3,500) → Executive(5,000) → Senior Executive(8,000) → CEO(12,000). Higher ranks multiply your click income up to 12x.

What are synergy bonuses?

Synergy bonuses activate when you own certain combinations of investments. For example, owning both financial products and real estate can trigger synergy effects that multiply your overall income beyond what each investment generates alone.

What is Kimchi Invasion?

Kimchi Invasion is a sci-fi factory automation idle game. Grow kimchi on Mars and export it to Earth. Manage production lines, optimize logistics, and scale your interplanetary kimchi empire. Currently in early access.

Technical

How is my progress saved?

Your progress is automatically saved to your browser's local storage every 5 seconds. If you sign in with Google, your progress is also synced to the cloud, allowing you to continue on any device.

Can I install it as an app (PWA)?

Yes! ClickSurvivor games support Progressive Web App (PWA) installation. On mobile, tap "Add to Home Screen" in your browser menu. On desktop Chrome, click the install icon in the address bar. The PWA works offline and receives automatic updates.

Does offline progress work?

Yes. When you return to the game after being away, it calculates the income you would have earned and adds it to your balance. The offline earnings are based on your passive income rate at the time you left.

What browsers are supported?

ClickSurvivor works on all modern browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, and Opera. We recommend the latest version for the best experience. Mobile browsers on iOS and Android are fully supported.

Account

Do I need an account to play?

No! You can play immediately without any account. All progress is saved locally in your browser. Creating an account (Google sign-in) is optional and only needed for cloud saves and cross-device sync.

How do I sync across devices?

Sign in with your Google account on each device. Your progress will automatically sync to the cloud. When you open the game on another device and sign in with the same account, your latest save will be loaded.

How do I delete my data?

To delete local data, clear your browser's site data for clicksurvivor.com. To delete your cloud data, go to Account settings and choose "Delete Account." This action is permanent and cannot be undone.
