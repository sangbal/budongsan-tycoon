About ClickSurvivor

Our Mission

ClickSurvivor is a free, ad-free gaming platform dedicated to delivering high-quality incremental, idle, and clicker games directly in your browser. We believe great games should be accessible to everyone without barriers. No downloads, no ads, no paywalls—just pure gaming enjoyment. Our mission is to create engaging experiences that respect your time and attention while offering meaningful progression systems and satisfying gameplay loops.

Our Games

Seoul Survival (서울 생존기)

A prestige-based incremental idle game set in modern Seoul. Start from humble beginnings and build your financial empire through strategic investments in property, stocks, and businesses. Master the art of resource management with our unique market event system, unlock powerful synergies, and discover dozens of achievements. Reset strategically to earn prestige points and unlock permanent bonuses that accelerate your next run. Features offline progress, cloud save support, and a deep progression system that rewards long-term planning.

Kimchi Invasion

A sci-fi factory automation game where you build and optimize kimchi production facilities on Mars. Combine incremental mechanics with automation systems to create the ultimate kimchi export empire. Design efficient production chains, research new technologies, and expand your interplanetary operations. Balance resource gathering, manufacturing, and logistics to maximize your kimchi output and unlock new planets to colonize.

Platform Features

  • 100% Free - No ads, no in-app purchases, no hidden costs. We're funded by passion, not paywalls.
  • Browser-Based - Instant play on any device. No downloads, no installation, no hassle.
  • PWA Support - Install as a native app on your device for a seamless offline experience.
  • Cloud Save - Sign in with Google to sync your progress across all your devices automatically.
  • Multilingual - Full support for Korean and English with more languages coming soon.
  • Offline Progress - Earn resources even when you're away. Your empire grows while you sleep.
